Woodwinds: choose an instrument

In woodwinds, a player directs air through a tube. Finger holes, keys, and reeds shape the vibrating air column.

Flute

A side-blown instrument with an ancient history and a clear, agile voice.

Saxophone

A single-reed instrument designed to blend the power of brass with woodwind color.

Bassoon

A folded double-reed instrument known for its broad range and expressive low register.

Clarinet

A single-reed instrument whose changing registers can sound both mellow and bright.
